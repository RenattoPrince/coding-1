Sport Climbing

Sport Climbing is a climbing style. Which you ascend while your clipping a rope into a pre-drilled metal bolts in a rock or a artificial wall. So it will save you if you fall.

Bouldering

Bouldering is a from of climbing. Where you can practice on small rocks and boulders in indoor walls. Typically the climbers won't go very high. They would jump down on a mat which obsorbs the fall.

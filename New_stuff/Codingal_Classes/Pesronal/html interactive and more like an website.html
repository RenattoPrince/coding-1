what are the diffrent types of Climbing?

Sport Climbing

Sport Climbing is a climbing style. Which you ascend while your clipping a rope into a pre-drilled metal bolts in a rock or a artificial wall. So it will save you if you fall.

Bouldering

Bouldering is a from of climbing. Where you can practice on small rocks and boulders in indoor walls. Typically the climbers won't go very high. They would jump down on a mat which obsorbs the fall.

Free Soloing

Free Soloing is when people climb on their own. Without a rope or crashpads. This Rsky type of climbing is only taken by experienced climbers.

High Balling

High Balling is like Bouldering but is basically free soloing and bouldering made a baby. No rope but most of the times using crashpads to save the fall.

Ice climbing

Ice Climbing is a form of climbing that is related with ice that you climb on using ice axes and crampons to grip the ice wall. To save you fall the ice climbers use ice screws that you can manually easily drill into a wall.

Competition climbing

Competition climbing is just literally a form of climbing that you litreally compete on artificial walls. Normally pro climbing Athletes compete these.
